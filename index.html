THE FIELD PROBLEM

Weather is changing.
Decisions cannot wait.

Farmers face rainfall shifts, heat stress and changing soil conditions that are difficult to see from the ground alone. FieldShift translates Earth-observation signals into a simple field snapshot — so users can spot conditions worth checking locally.
